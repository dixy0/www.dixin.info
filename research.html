Research

My work centers on how brain regions interact, measured mainly with fMRI, and how those interactions change across tasks, development and disorders.

Themes

Brain alterations in autism

Brain mechanisms of autism spectrum disorder, including white matter integrity and temporal lobe volume.

Connectivity during movie watching

Measuring dynamic and stationary connectivity during naturalistic viewing, from childhood to early adulthood.

Connectivity across task conditions

How connectivity and network organization shift between rest, tasks and movies, using PPI, beta series, sliding window and intersubject correlation.

Modulation by other regions

How a third region changes the connectivity between two others, in resting-state and task data.

Metabolic & molecular connectivity

Metabolic connectivity from FDG-PET, including effects of aging. I chair the Outreach Council of the Molecular Connectivity Working Group.
