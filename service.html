Molecular Connectivity Working Group

Chair, Outreach Council · 2022–present

The Molecular Connectivity Working Group (MCWG) is an international initiative that evaluates molecular imaging for studying brain connectivity. As chair of its Outreach Council, I organize the Molecular Connectivity Online Series (MCOS): free monthly online seminars on molecular connectivity research, methods tutorials and related connectivity findings, each with a talk followed by open discussion.

I presented MCOS011 (March 2025), on intra- versus inter-individual correlational connectivity, and organized the symposium “Relationship between Brain Energy Consumption and Brain Organizations” at OHBM 2025 in Brisbane.
